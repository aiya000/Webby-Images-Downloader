Webby Images Downloader lists the images on a web page, lets you pick the ones you want by tapping, and downloads them all at once.

Share a page URL to the app from your browser, or paste it into the URL field. The app collects every image on the page and shows them in a grid, numbered in the order they appear on the page. Tap the images you want, tap Download, and they are saved to Download/WebbyImagesDownloader/ with their original file names.

Features:

• Receives a URL via the Android share sheet, or takes one pasted into the URL field
• Collects images from img tags (including lazy-load attributes), srcset, picture sources, and og:image / twitter:image meta tags
• Numbered grid with select-all and clear-selection buttons
• Downloads selected images in one batch through Android's download manager, so they keep going after you leave the app
• Notifies you when the batch finishes, including how many failed
• Built-in full-screen viewer with swipe, pinch to zoom, double-tap, and quick-scale gestures
• Light and dark theme

The app has no trackers and no analytics. The only network access is to the page URL you enter and to the images on it.

The UI is currently in Japanese only.
